01 / Normal groups

Arrive ready to enjoy the company.

Normal groups won’t climb Monte Grappa. If you’re wondering how to get ready for several days on the bike, think in four phases rather than targets on a calendar.

  1. 01 · Months out

    Make room for a ride

    Choose a loop you look forward to or a friend you like riding with. A rhythm that fits your life is easier to keep.

  2. 02 · Build comfort

    Range a little farther

    When your usual ride feels familiar, explore a longer way home. Notice which snacks, clothes and saddle setup still feel good near the end.

  3. 03 · Consecutive days

    Go out again tomorrow

    Try riding on neighbouring days. The question isn’t how fast you finish the first ride, but how it feels to roll out for the second.

  4. 04 · Ease back

    Leave something in hand

    As departure approaches, let the rides get easier. Give yourself time to rest, check the bike and pack the kit you trust.

02 / Long groups

From Semonzo to Monte Grappa

Long groups will climb Monte Grappa; the exact route is unconfirmed. Follow the bends above Semonzo for a sense of the mountain’s scale. This is one possible approach, not the event course.
