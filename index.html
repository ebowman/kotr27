02 / Week one · Consistency

Find a gear you can repeat.

Choose a familiar, quiet stretch where you can pedal without chasing speed. Flat ground or an indoor trainer works just as well as a gentle rise.

Your first ride

Start with 8 minutes of easy spinning. Pick one goal below, using either the feel cue or your usual power zones. Finish with 5 minutes easy.

Social goal · Keep the conversation going

Ride steadily for 5 minutes, spin easily for 3, then repeat the steady 5. Shift down before the road tilts so a companion never has to break off mid-story.

By feel: You can answer in full sentences, even near the end. By power: Stay in low Zone 2 (endurance); recover in Zone 1 (easy).

Short on time? Do one steady 5 before cooling down. Success is finishing ready for another conversation.

Performance goal · Match two efforts

Ride 6 minutes at a controlled tempo. Spin lightly for 4 minutes, then ride another 6 at the same effort. Resist the urge to surge in the final minute.

By feel: Breathing deepens but short phrases come comfortably; the second finish should feel like the first. By power: Hold low Zone 3 (tempo) on both efforts, with Zone 1 between.

Short on time? Make the efforts 3 minutes each and keep the full recovery. Aim to match them, not beat the first.
