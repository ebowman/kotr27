What is FTP?

Functional threshold power is an estimate of the power you can sustain in a hard, steady effort. If you know it in watts, you can use it here without entering weight.

    Does the climbing work change?

    The same rider and kit rise the same height, so the work against gravity is unchanged. Work at the pedals also covers rolling resistance, air drag and drivetrain losses. Drag increases with speed; a stationary stop adds no pedal work.

      About this route & model

      This is an example ascent from Semonzo; the event route is not confirmed. Elevation is exaggerated: screen angle isn’t road grade. Positions are modeled from grade windows, not GPS fixes or event predictions.

      Another climb’s time, length and gain alone can’t tell us sustained power: slopes, stops and wind can differ. Without FTP, we keep Robin’s example power rather than guessing yours.
