About the diamond baseline

◇ Hollow diamond = pinned strategy; ● solid circle = live rider. Distance is live minus baseline at equal elapsed time from this trial’s origin. Arrival seconds and mechanical pedal kJ are live minus baseline at the common distance both have reached, before any stop there. Negative seconds means live arrived sooner, not a ranking.

A pin freezes route revision, rider/model, origin, stops, initial controls and applied actions; then holds the last effort/gear/coast. Pending commands are excluded: press Play to apply them before pinning. Seek, restart, route or rider/model changes clear the pin with a reason; live effort/gear choices do not. Repin replaces it without seeking. Nothing is saved or fetched.

Estimates in constant conditions, no momentum or fatigue; uphill coasting stalls immediately. No future arrival is promised for a stalled baseline. Bounded to 24 ride hours, 1,024 applied actions, 2,048 windows and 8,192 indexed segments; beyond these limits start a shorter trial. Rider, drivetrain and still-air assumptions are in Setup.

Survey sections

    Algorithmic sections · processed averages from 250 m windows (the final window may be longer), not measured road gradients. Elevation errors can affect gain and grade. No surface, hazards or recovery guarantees.
